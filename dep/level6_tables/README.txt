Notation in the files:
GR[a[1],...,a[w]] stands for the real part of G[{a[1],...,a[w]}, 1]
and 
GI[a[1],...,a[w]] stands for the imaginary part of G[{a[1],...,a[w]}, 1]


Files with reduction to irreducible elements for weights 1,2,...,6 are
sl1re.m, sl1im.m
sl2re.m, sl2im.m
sl3re.m, sl3im.m
sl4re.m, sl4im.m
sl5re.m, sl5im.m
sl6re.m, sl6im.m
After loading them, one obtains irRe[1],irIm[1],...,irRe[6],irIm[6]
as the elements of the bases.
