# Third-party notices

This release vendors only the files in the runtime closure of the Standard route. The original notices and
license files retained in the bundle are authoritative. Packages published without a licence that permits
redistribution are not part of the repository: `make` downloads them from their authors' pages (below).

- PolyLogTools 1.4 (Claude Duhr, Falko Dulat; arXiv:1904.07279): NOT redistributed. `make` downloads its files,
  listed in the `Makefile`, from https://gitlab.com/pltteam/plt at commit e0284d0cba0299f6d584094a053d3e890d13aebe
  into `dep/PolyLogTools/`, checks them against `dep/UPSTREAM.sha256` and applies `dep/PolyLogTools.patch`, which
  loads the project's two files `core/MZVw78.m` (the weight-7/8 multiple-zeta values) and `core/ReduceCG78.m` (the
  weight 7--10 branch of `ReduceCG`, a fail-closed identity reached by no route), adds the same fail-closed identity
  for weight 6, makes the `GiNaC` interface accept words given as lists, lets the numerical check of `Fiber` tolerate
  rounding (and skip when `ginsh` is unavailable), and renames the internal `HermiteReduce` of `core/PSLQ.m`, which
  Mathematica 15 makes a protected System symbol.
- HPL 2.0 (Daniel Maitre; hep-ph/0507152, hep-ph/0703052) and HypExp 2.0 (Tobias Huber, Daniel Maitre;
  hep-ph/0507094, arXiv:0708.2443): NOT redistributed. `make` downloads `HPL-2.0.tar.gz` and `HypExp-2.0.tar.gz`
  from https://www.physik.uzh.ch/data/HPL/ and https://www.physik.uzh.ch/data/HypExp/, checks them against
  `dep/UPSTREAM.sha256`, unpacks the files listed in the `Makefile` into `dep/HPL` and `dep/HypExp`, and applies
  `dep/HPL/HPL.m.patch` (the MZV tables are read up to weight 10, and `hpl910_constants.m` is loaded after `nmzv.m`).
  The project's own files in `dep/HPL` are the minimal sets and MZV tables of weight 7 to 10 and
  `hpl910_constants.m`, generated by the MBAnalytica authors, those of weight 9 and 10 from the bases of the MZV data
  mine (J. Bluemlein, D. J. Broadhurst, J. A. M. Vermaseren, arXiv:0907.2557); they are released under CC BY 4.0, and
  the large ones are attached to the release hpl-tables-v1 and downloaded by `make`.
- HyperIntica (SubTropica): M. Giroux, S. Mizera and G. Salvatori, *SubTropica*, arXiv:2604.20954; a native
  Mathematica reimplementation of E. Panzer's HyperInt (arXiv:1403.3385).  See `dep/HyperIntica/LICENSE`,
  `dep/HyperIntica/LICENSE-DATA` and `dep/HyperIntica/README.md`.  Used by MB2MPL's IntVolFLINT strategy as
  the exact evaluator of hyperlogarithm words.
- IntVolFLINT, a fork of HyperFLINT 1.2.13 (SubTropica; M. Giroux, S. Mizera, G. Salvatori; MIT license): the fork's
  additions (marked `// IVF:` in the source) are by Syed Mehedi Hasan; the upstream code and its licence are retained:
  `dep/IntVolFLINT/LICENSE`, `dep/IntVolFLINT/THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES`, `dep/IntVolFLINT/FORK_PROVENANCE.md`;
  source in `dep/IntVolFLINT/src`, build notes in `dep/IntVolFLINT/BUILD.md`.
- Henn--Smirnov--Smirnov tables of multiple polylogarithm values at sixth roots of unity (arXiv:1512.08389),
  weights 1--4 only: `dep/level6_tables/PROVENANCE.md`.
- MBConicHulls 1.3 (arXiv:2012.15108, 2212.11839, 2309.00409,
  2512.19803); GPL-3.0, see `dep/MBConicHulls/LICENSE`.  Used by the SymbolicSum method for the series representations.
- MB.m 1.4 (M. Czakon; hep-ph/0511200, updated by A. V. Belitsky, A. V. Smirnov, V. A. Smirnov), MBresolve.m
  (A. V. Smirnov, V. A. Smirnov; arXiv:0901.0386) and barnesroutines.m (D. A. Kosower): NOT redistributed. `make`
  downloads them, unchanged, from https://gitlab.com/feynmanintegrals/MB at commit
  5ddd820144c1e50c3e1d35f21ad6f0d167dbff20 into `dep/MB/` and checks them against `dep/UPSTREAM.sha256`.
- DIOGENES (M. A. Bezuglov, A. I. Onishchenko, arXiv:2312.06242; GPL-3.0) is NOT shipped.  The multifold handler's
  `multifold_close/mbeDiogenesFallback.m` is an optional hook for its differential-equation expansion of a
  hypergeometric block; it installs only when a DIOGENES package directory (`DIOGENES.wl`) is present at
  `Global`$MBStandardDiogenesPath` or `dep/DIOGENES/`, and no value of the record depends on it.

GiNaC/CLN (the `ginsh` shell used by PolyLogTools for numerics) and Mathematica are external prerequisites and are
not part of this bundle.
